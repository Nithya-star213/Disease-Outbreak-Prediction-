What is PulseWatch?

PulseWatch is a real-time disease outbreak intelligence platform developed to monitor health signals and provide early warnings for disease outbreaks across India. We use advanced language models (BioBERT) to help structure symptom descriptions and clinical histories into possible differential diagnosis hypotheses.

The goal is to support—not replace—public health officials and medical professionals: help organize information, surface relevant possibilities, and improve early outbreak detection times from the first signal. Key features include structured diagnostic support with prioritized hypotheses, GDPR-ready architecture with automatic anonymization, and real-time data streaming.

How does PulseWatch work?

PulseWatch works in 4 simple steps:

1
Describe your symptoms by entering a medical description in free text.
2
Intelligent processing: Our AI algorithms analyze the information using advanced language models, detecting clinical patterns and medical correlations.
3
Differential diagnosis support: You receive a prioritized list of possible diagnostic hypotheses, each with information about compatible symptoms, suggested evaluation areas, and case analysis.
4
Interactive refinement: You can answer AI-generated follow-up questions to refine the clinical description, obtain information about specific diseases, or request genetic testing suggestions to discuss with a healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my medical information kept confidential?
Yes, your privacy is our highest priority. PulseWatch is an initiative committed to rigorously protecting your personal and health data. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. All data you provide is securely encrypted both in transit and at rest.
Can I use PulseWatch in emergency situations?
No, PulseWatch is not intended or designed to handle medical emergencies or urgent medical conditions. In the event of an emergency, please immediately contact your local emergency services (Call 108 Ambulance / 1056 Helpline) or visit the nearest emergency facility. PulseWatch is developed as a supportive resource for medical professionals and public health monitoring.
How does PulseWatch suggest possible diagnostic hypotheses?
PulseWatch utilizes advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning models (BioBERT NLP) to analyze symptoms and clinical information provided by healthcare professionals. It suggests possible diagnostic hypotheses and relevant clinical information to support differential diagnosis. PulseWatch is intended to support qualified healthcare professionals and public health officials.
What data sources does PulseWatch use?
PulseWatch processes data from multiple public sources: Reddit health discussions, WHO RSS feeds, Google Trends aggregated search behavior, and NewsAPI public health news reports.
Is PulseWatch free to use?
Yes, PulseWatch is entirely free to use. It is developed and maintained as a non-profit project, funded through collaborative partnerships. We aim to make advanced AI diagnostic support tools freely available to healthcare professionals and public health officials globally.
Is my data used for secondary use?
PulseWatch never uses your personal data for secondary purposes such as marketing or commercial activities. We strictly use aggregated, anonymous data—including general usage patterns, frequently reported symptoms, common diagnostic suggestions, and overall trends—to enhance our algorithms' accuracy, reliability, and effectiveness.
