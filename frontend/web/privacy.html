PulseWatch Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026 • Platform Developer: KaviyaRekha

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

PulseWatch ("PulseWatch", "we", "our", "us") is a real-time disease outbreak intelligence platform developed by KaviyaRekha. As a public health surveillance system, we prioritize data privacy and advocate for the responsible handling of health-related data.

Data Protection Officer (DPO): privacy@pulsewatch.io
General Enquiries: support@pulsewatch.io
Lead Developer: KaviyaRekha (S.A. Engineering College AI&DS)

2. Regulatory & Clinical Status

PulseWatch processes publicly available data from sources such as Reddit health discussions, WHO RSS feeds, Google Trends, and NewsAPI. The platform does not collect or process patient-specific medical records or personally identifiable information (PII). PulseWatch is designed to support qualified health professionals and public health monitoring, and must not be relied upon as a substitute for professional medical judgment or diagnosis.

3. Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

PulseWatch operates on a strict zero-PII architecture. We do not store user names, home addresses, phone numbers, or un-hashed IP addresses. IP addresses reaching our edge network are immediately salted and hashed via SHA-256 before any analytical logging occurs.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR (Articles 15–22)

Although PulseWatch collects no PII, users retain full rights to inquire about system processing policies, request data deletion of any submitted feedback logs, or opt out of analytics cookies by contacting privacy@pulsewatch.io.
